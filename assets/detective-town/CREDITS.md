# Detective Town artwork

Portraits are downloaded from DiceBear's Adventurer collection, a remix of **Adventurer by Lisa Wischofsky**, licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**.

- Collection and attribution: https://www.dicebear.com/styles/adventurer/
- Original work: https://www.figma.com/community/file/1184595184137881796
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source endpoint: `https://api.dicebear.com/9.x/adventurer/svg?seed=NAME&backgroundColor=b6c7b6`
- Seeds: mara, ellis, tomas, inez, rowan, ada, felix, nora, leon, sana, hugo, vera.

Avatar combinations and background colours were selected for this game; the supplied SVG illustrations have not otherwise been edited. Files are hosted locally. Visible attribution is included in the game's footer. The downloader is `tools/download-detective-town-art.cjs`.

No artwork or characters from Blood on the Clocktower or Demon Bluff are included.
