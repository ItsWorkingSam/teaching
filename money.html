Everyday money maths with NZ prices: counting coins, change, specials, pay and GST.

How it works
  • Type the amount, like 4.50. The $ is already there.
  • Use paper or a calculator if it helps. That's what you'd do at the shops.
  • Get 5 right to move up a level.
  • Miss one and you'll see how to work it out.
