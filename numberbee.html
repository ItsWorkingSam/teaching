Number Bee

Mental maths that gets harder as you go, from adding to 20 up to percentages and GST.

How it works
  • Work it out in your head, or on paper if you like.
  • Type just the number. You can leave out $, % and commas.
  • Practice stays at your chosen level for as long as you like.
  • Miss one and you'll see a quick way to work it out.
  • Choose Level up to move on after 5 correct answers, or Challenge for three lives.
